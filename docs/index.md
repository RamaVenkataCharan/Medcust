# Privacy Policy for MedTrack Mobile (Medcust)

**Effective Date:** September 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 15, 2026  

MedTrack Mobile ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed under the Medcust project, is a standalone, offline digital khata (ledger) utility designed for local retail and medical shop owners to track customer purchase entries, medicine notes, and due balances directly on their personal device.

---

### 1. Data Collection and Storage
- **100% On-Device Storage:** All customer records (name, phone number, village, optional address), purchase entries, medicine items, and payment amounts entered into MedTrack Mobile are stored strictly and exclusively on your device's local SQLite database.
- **No Remote Servers or Cloud Sync:** MedTrack Mobile does not operate any backend servers, cloud databases, or tracking infrastructure. We do not receive, transmit, intercept, or sell your data.
- **No Telemetry or Tracking:** The app does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or background trackers.

---

### 2. Permissions & Data Sharing
- **Storage / File Sharing:** When you explicitly choose to use the "Export Backup" feature, the app generates a local JSON backup file and opens your device's native sharing sheet. You retain full control over where that file is shared (e.g., your personal Google Drive, WhatsApp, or local file manager). The app itself does not transmit files anywhere without your direct action.
- **No Third-Party Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, or share user data with any third parties or marketing platforms.

---

### 3. Data Retention and Deletion
- Since all data is stored exclusively on your device, deleting customer entries or uninstalling the app permanently removes all stored data from the device. We have no ability to recover or delete data on your behalf.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
- MedTrack Mobile is intended for business operators and retail merchants, and does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Security
- Because your data remains solely on your physical device, security is safeguarded by your device's operating system security controls (passcode, biometric authentication, and sandboxed application storage).

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
- Any updates to this policy will be published directly to this page with an updated revision date.
